# 08 · Report

`SUMMARY.md` for each study, in this order:

1. **Headline**: one sentence with the effect size and interval for the primary comparison, and whether it is
   statistically reliable under 04.
2. **Caveats that change the headline**, before anything else (e.g. home-ground tasks, underpowered).
3. **Every pre-registered prediction** with held / not held / not testable, including nulls.
4. **Per arm table** (pass^1 with cluster intervals, pass^k), **paired comparisons** (difference, interval, p, Holm p).
5. **Mechanism**: what the transcripts show about why (with counts, not anecdotes alone).
6. **In-scope vs out-of-scope** results separately.
7. **Limits** and what the study can and cannot detect (03).
8. **Reproduce**: commit, command, seeds.

Plain words, numbers in tables, no "significant" without the test behind it.
